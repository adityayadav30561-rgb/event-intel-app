# Event Intel: getting started

A one-page guide for the team. Takes about three minutes.

## 1. Install the app

**iPhone** (iOS 16.4 or newer)
1. Open **https://event-intelligence-india.expo.app** in **Safari** (not Chrome; only Safari can install it).
2. Tap **Share** (the square with an arrow) → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
3. From now on, open **Event Intel** from its home-screen icon. Alerts and offline use work only there, not in a Safari tab.

**Android**
1. Open the same link in **Chrome**.
2. Tap **Install app** (or menu **⋮** → **Add to Home screen**).

## 2. Sign in

Your admin sends you an email address and a **temporary password**. Sign in, then choose your own password (at least 10 characters). Nobody else, including the admin, can see it.

## 3. Set up (first launch)

- **Interests:** pick the technologies, topics and cities you care about. They decide the "Strong match" badges and the For You list. Change them any time in More → Interests.
- **Alerts:** tap **Turn On Alerts** and allow notifications to hear about changes to events you follow, new matches for your saved searches, and your reminders. On iPhone this works only in the installed app. More → Notifications → **Send a Test** checks it.

## 4. Everyday use

| To… | Do this |
|---|---|
| Find events | **Explore**: type naturally ("SAP events in Delhi next month"), or use Location, Dates and Filters. **Show Map** shows them on a map. |
| Keep an event | **Save** it, or **Follow** it for alerts when its date, venue or status changes. |
| Plan a visit | On the event: **Visit** (planning → confirmed → visited), visit day, travel notes, checklist, note. |
| Get reminded | **Remind Me** (a week, 3 days, a day or 2 hours before). **Add to Calendar** puts it in your phone's calendar. |
| On the day | Home shows **Today** → **Event Day**: hours, directions, your checklist and note. |
| No signal at the venue | On the event, turn on **Available Offline** beforehand. Saves, notes and checklists made offline sync later by themselves. |

## 5. Good to know

- **Updates** install themselves the next time you open the app. More shows the version and build date.
- **Privacy:** your notes are yours. On an event, the team sees only who plans to go or went, never your notes or checklists.
- **Scope:** this app is for finding and visiting events. Leads and contacts stay in the team's Excel workflow.
- **Problems?** Close the app fully and open it again. If it still fails, tell the admin what the screen says.
